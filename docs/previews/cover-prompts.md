# README cover prompts

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool. The initial English image establishes the composition; Chinese localizes the same image. The final two editing prompts apply regular book-serif typography in English and upright Songti in Chinese while preserving the palette and storyboard. These are conceptual storyboard illustrations, not application screenshots or research results.

## English

Use case: ads-marketing / technical editorial illustration.
Create an original wide GitHub README cover for "Research Presentation", a Codex skill that turns research projects and paper PDFs into evidence-grounded HTML talks. Wide 2.4:1 canvas, approximately 1920 by 800. This project's identity should evoke a projected research talk and a considered storyboard.
Palette: deep midnight navy #18243C as the dominant background, cool near-white #EFF3FB for type and slide surfaces, crisp blue #4776C8 for secondary linework, and restrained coral-orange #F38167 for focus. Absolutely no green in this cover. Clean geometric sans-serif typography, large and legible. No serif book typography, no paper texture, no desk props.
Composition: left half is a strong typographic title block with a small coral rule; right half is an architectural storyboard of THREE landscape 16:9 slide frames, offset horizontally and vertically so all three large labels are visible. The frames feel like projected slides, with straight edges, fine blue rules, subtle depth and controlled generous negative space. Integrate the frames into one composition, avoid a grid of dashboard cards.
The three frames contain only simple abstract content marks: one large title rule under Question; two rectangles and a short connector under Mechanism; several aligned source-note rules under Evidence. These are conceptual motifs, not real app screenshots or scientific findings. No graphs, axes, experimental values, fake citations or results. No people, logos, seals, buttons or browser chrome.
Exact text only, English:
Left main title, across two balanced lines: "Research" then "Presentation"
Left short subtitle: "A clear story. Grounded in evidence."
Three large slide-frame labels on the right: "Question", "Mechanism", "Evidence"
No extra words. Make the title dominant and the three labels readable at GitHub article width. Refined, confident, presentation-oriented; distinct from a green manuscript toolkit cover.
