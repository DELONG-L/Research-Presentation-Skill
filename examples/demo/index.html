The argument

02 / 03

Give every slide an argumentative job

Choose a role to see how it moves a research story forward.

QuestionMechanismEvidence

What makes the problem worth solving?

Introduce the problem, the setting, and the boundary. Give the audience a reason to care before introducing the method.

What the audience should leave withA precise question and the conditions under which it matters.

How does the method support its claim?

Connect the inputs, design choices, and claimed property. Make the reasoning visible instead of relying on a method name.

What the audience should leave withA traceable explanation from design choice to claimed property.

What can the evidence actually establish?

Show the observations that support the claim. Keep uncertainty, assumptions, and limitations next to the result.

What the audience should leave withA bounded conclusion that goes no further than its source.

Workflow guidance, not scientific findings.
