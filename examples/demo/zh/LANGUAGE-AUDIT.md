# Language And Claim Audit

## Canonical Terminology

研究汇报 is the Chinese display name for Research Presentation. Use 草稿叙事 for draft narrative and 确认后的叙事 for confirmed narrative. 问题、机制、证据 correspond to the three roles in the English example.

## Claim Strength

The three slides localize the English working example. They describe capabilities from public skill files, not empirical research findings. The same claim IDs and source roles are retained.

## Prohibited Claims

No claims about scientific effectiveness, fabricated results, or unrecorded narrative confirmation.

## Final Consistency Check

The localized titles and unchanged claim IDs are recorded in the local manifest. This edition retains the original stylesheet and navigation script, with Chinese control labels. Runtime resources remain local.
